About

I earned a B.S. in Community Psychology from Georgia State University and worked as an IRB-approved researcher on the Grady Trauma Project at Emory University, studying trauma and resilience. Coaching is how I learned to move forward, through recovery, grief and the end of a long marriage, so it's what I do now.

The goal hasn't changed: help high-achieving adults with ADHD and AuDHD stop surviving, understand how their brains actually work, and build lives designed around their strengths.
